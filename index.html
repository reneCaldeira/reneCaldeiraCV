01 · About

Two disciplines, one eye.

Hi — I’m Renê, a multidisciplinary designer & photographer with 8+ years of experience across branding, editorial and commercial photography.

My practice sits between two chairs: a print-tuned obsession with typography, grids and systems, and a photographer’s instinct for light, composition and honest moments. Both feed each other — and both start from the same question: what do we want people to feel?

“Good work happens when design and photography are planned together — not bolted together later.”
